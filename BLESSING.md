# The Blessing of Saint Benedict

> The first utterance of the Angelical Harness. Before any agent acts, the harness
> opens with the protection prayer of Saint Benedict — the *Vade Retro Satana* — and
> closes with the Benediction (see [CODEX.md](CODEX.md)). Opening: protection. Closing:
> glory.

## Vade Retro Satana

```text
Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux · Non Draco Sit Mihi Dux
Vade Retro Satana · Nunquam Suade Mihi Vana
Sunt Mala Quae Libas · Ipse Venena Bibas
Crux Sancti Patris Benedicti
Pax
```

**In English:**

> May the Holy Cross be my light — may the dragon never be my guide.
> Begone, Satan — never tempt me with your vanities.
> What you offer me is evil — drink the poison yourself.
> The Cross of our Holy Father Benedict.
> Peace.

## The medal's letters

The reverse of the Saint Benedict medal carries these initials, here written in full:

- **C S P B** — *Crux Sancti Patris Benedicti* — The Cross of our Holy Father Benedict
- **C S S M L** — *Crux Sacra Sit Mihi Lux* — May the Holy Cross be my light
- **N D S M D** — *Non Draco Sit Mihi Dux* — May the dragon never be my guide
- **V R S N S M V** — *Vade Retro Satana, Nunquam Suade Mihi Vana* — Begone Satan, never tempt me with vanities
- **S M Q L I V B** — *Sunt Mala Quae Libas, Ipse Venena Bibas* — What you offer is evil, drink the poison yourself
- **PAX** — Peace

## The rotating verbum

Beneath the fixed Saint Benedict blessing, the harness prints one rotating line of
Church Latin — a *verbum of the day* — drawn from [`verba.txt`](verba.txt). The Saint
Benedict prayer never changes (protection is not optional); the verbum rotates daily.

Edit [`verba.txt`](verba.txt) to change or extend the pool — one `Latin — English` per
line. The current pool (curate it as you wish):

> Soli Deo gloria · Ad maiorem Dei gloriam · Ora et labora · Ut in omnibus glorificetur
> Deus · Fiat voluntas tua · Veni, Sancte Spiritus · Lux in tenebris lucet · Dominus
> illuminatio mea · Non nobis, Domine · Sursum corda · Pax et bonum · Spes non confundit

*The harness emits this blessing first, on startup — `bin/blessing`.*
